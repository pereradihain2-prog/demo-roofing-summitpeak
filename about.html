GAF Master Elite Explained

What Does GAF Master Elite Actually Mean — And Why Should You Care?

GAF is the largest roofing manufacturer in North America. Their Master Elite program requires contractors to maintain proper licensing, adequate insurance, pass a quality test, and demonstrate a commitment to ongoing training. Less than 2% of roofing contractors in Canada qualify.

Why does it matter to you? Three reasons:

  • 01. Longer warranties. Only Master Elite contractors can offer the Golden Pledge warranty — GAF's best. Regular contractors max out at the standard limited warranty.
  • 02. Verifiable quality. The certification is publicly verifiable on GAF's website. It can't be faked. If they say they're Master Elite, you can check.
  • 03. Higher insurance value. GAF-certified installations are viewed more favorably by insurance companies because the materials and installation meet a documented standard.
