## 8. SERVICES — 4-Line Formula Seeds
 
1. **Full Roof Replacement**
   - Pain: Roof is 20+ years old, shingles curling, or you're getting water inside — and you don't know if insurance will cover it
   - Solution: Complete tear-off and replacement with premium architectural shingles, typically done in one day
   - Differentiator: GAF Master Elite certified — we carry the longest warranties available. Most jobs covered fully or partially by insurance.
   - CTA: "Get My Free Replacement Quote"
2. **Roof Repair & Leak Fix**
   - Pain: You see water stains on your ceiling or notice missing shingles after a storm — and you're not sure if it's a small fix or a bigger problem
   - Solution: Same-week diagnostic inspection and repair — we fix the source, not just the symptom
   - Differentiator: Written repair warranty. If the leak returns within 2 years, we come back at no charge.
   - CTA: "Book My Repair Inspection"
3. **Emergency Tarping & Storm Response**
   - Pain: A storm just hit and your roof is exposed. Every hour of delay means more water damage, more mold risk, more cost.
   - Solution: 24/7 emergency tarping to protect your home until permanent repairs can be scheduled
   - Differentiator: We document everything for your insurance claim during the emergency visit — photos, measurements, damage report.
   - CTA: "Call for Emergency Tarping Now"
4. **Insurance Claim Assistance**
   - Pain: Dealing with insurance adjusters is confusing, slow, and you're worried about getting underpaid or denied
   - Solution: We work directly with your insurance company — we prepare the damage documentation, communicate with the adjuster, and make sure you get what you're owed
   - Differentiator: We've processed hundreds of Alberta insurance claims. We know what adjusters look for and how to document damage correctly.
   - CTA: "Get My Insurance Claim Started"
5. **Free Roof Inspection**
   - Pain: You're not sure if your roof needs replacing, repairing, or is fine for another few years — and you don't want to be sold something you don't need
   - Solution: Honest, no-pressure inspection with a written report — we'll tell you exactly what condition your roof is in and what it'll cost to fix
   - Differentiator: Our inspectors are salaried, not commissioned. Zero incentive to upsell you.
   - CTA: "Book My Free Inspection"
6. **Eavestrough & Soffit Repair**
   - Pain: Sagging, leaking, or broken eavestroughs are routing water into your foundation and fascia — and most people don't notice until it's a major problem
   - Solution: Full eavestrough inspection, cleaning, repair, or replacement — done same-day with most roofing jobs
   - Differentiator: We don't subcontract eavestrough work. Our crew handles it directly, and it's covered by the same workmanship warranty.
   - CTA: "Add Eavestrough to My Quote"

## 16. DESIGN NOTES — ROOFING SPECIFIC
 
- **Dark, serious palette** — charcoal + steel blue + amber. This is NOT a friendly green landscaping site. It's serious and authoritative.
- **Emergency elements should pop** — the 24/7 storm damage banner needs to be impossible to miss. Use red or amber with high contrast.
- **Certifications should be displayed like badges** — not just text. GAF Master Elite, BBB, ARCA should look like real credentials.
- **Insurance section needs its own visual weight** — this is the biggest differentiator. Give it a dedicated section with a dark background to separate it from the rest.
- **Before/after pairs matter** — roofing before/after is the most powerful trust-builder in this industry. Make them large and prominent.
- **Financing callout should appear near pricing** — "$0 down" removes the #1 objection for high-ticket work.
- **Avoid: bright colors, playful fonts, rounded everything** — this needs to feel like a company that's been doing serious work for 20 years.
